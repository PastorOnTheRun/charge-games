How it works

Three steps. Two minutes.

  1. Open the game on the TV

    Any smart TV browser, or a laptop plugged into the TV or projector with HDMI.

  2. Scan the QR with your phone

    The leader’s phone becomes the controller: spin, award points, undo, change settings.

  3. Split into tables and play

    Bibles out, eyes on the screen. The first table to find it wins the points.

FAQ

Questions

Can students join with their phones?

No. Only the leader’s phone connects, as the controller. Students play with Bibles at their tables, so nobody needs a phone, an account, or an app.

Does it work offline?

No. The screen and the phone talk over the internet, so both need a connection (Wi-Fi or cell data). If the connection drops, scores are saved on the screen and the phone reconnects on its own.

Which Bible translation does it use?

None. The games show references only (like “Romans 8:28”), never the verse text, so any translation your group reads works.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. Open the game in a browser and scan the code. Nothing to install and no sign-in.
