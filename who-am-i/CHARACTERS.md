# Who Am I? characters (review list)

Source: `characters.js` (the game reads that file; this list is for review only). 32 characters, 5 clues each, hardest first. Clues are short paraphrases, not quotations. The answer shows one CSB reference (reference only, no verse text).

With 3 or 4 clues per character, the game uses the last 3 or 4 (the easier ones).

Notes on clues that are worth a second look are at the end.

## 1. Adam (OT) · answer reference: Genesis 2:7

1. I lived 930 years.
2. My third son named in Scripture was Seth.
3. God brought every animal to me to see what I would name it.
4. God made my wife from one of my ribs.
5. I was the first man, placed in the garden of Eden.

## 2. Noah (OT) · answer reference: Genesis 6:13-14

1. My father was Lamech.
2. After the disaster I planted a vineyard.
3. My sons were Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
4. I sent out a raven and then a dove to look for dry land.
5. I built an ark and survived the flood.

## 3. Abraham (OT) · answer reference: Genesis 12:1-3

1. I gave a tenth of everything to Melchizedek, king of Salem.
2. I came from Ur of the Chaldeans.
3. God made my name longer when he made a covenant with me.
4. My son was born when I was 100 years old.
5. My wife was Sarah and my son of the promise was Isaac.

## 4. Jacob (OT) · answer reference: Genesis 32:28

1. I set up a stone pillar at Bethel after a dream about a stairway to heaven.
2. I worked fourteen years for my father-in-law Laban to marry his daughters.
3. I wrestled all night and was given the new name Israel.
4. I traded a bowl of stew for my brother's birthright.
5. I am Esau's twin, and I tricked my father Isaac to get the blessing.

## 5. Joseph (OT) · answer reference: Genesis 41:39-41

1. I married Asenath, daughter of a priest of On.
2. I was put in charge of everything in Potiphar's house.
3. In prison I explained the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker.
4. My jealous brothers sold me to traders headed for Egypt.
5. My father Jacob gave me a special robe, and I dreamed my brothers bowed to me.

## 6. Moses (OT) · answer reference: Exodus 3:10

1. My father-in-law Jethro told me to appoint leaders to help me judge the people.
2. My mother hid me for three months when I was a baby.
3. I killed an Egyptian and ran away to Midian.
4. God spoke to me from a bush that was on fire but did not burn up.
5. I led Israel out of Egypt and brought down the Ten Commandments.

## 7. Joshua (OT) · answer reference: Joshua 6:20

1. I am the son of Nun.
2. When I prayed at Gibeon, the sun stood still.
3. I was one of only two spies who said Israel could take the land.
4. I led Israel after Moses died.
5. The walls of Jericho fell after we marched around the city.

## 8. Rahab (OT) · answer reference: Joshua 2:1

1. I am named in Jesus' family line in Matthew 1.
2. My house was built into the city wall.
3. I hid two men under stalks of flax on my roof.
4. I tied a scarlet cord in my window.
5. I hid Israel's spies in Jericho, and my family was saved when the city fell.

## 9. Deborah (OT) · answer reference: Judges 4:4-5

1. My husband was Lappidoth.
2. I held court under a palm tree between Ramah and Bethel.
3. I sang a victory song with Barak.
4. I told Barak to go fight Sisera's army.
5. I was a prophetess who judged Israel.

## 10. Gideon (OT) · answer reference: Judges 7:7

1. I was also called Jerubbaal.
2. An angel found me threshing wheat in a winepress.
3. I asked God for a sign with a wool fleece, wet and then dry.
4. God cut my army down to 300 men.
5. My men blew trumpets, broke jars, and held up torches to defeat Midian.

## 11. Samson (OT) · answer reference: Judges 16:28-30

1. I told a riddle about honey I found in a lion's carcass.
2. I tied torches to foxes' tails to burn the Philistines' fields.
3. I killed a thousand men with a donkey's jawbone.
4. I was a Nazirite, and my hair was never to be cut.
5. Delilah found out the secret of my strength.

## 12. Ruth (OT) · answer reference: Ruth 1:16

1. My first husband was Mahlon.
2. I came from Moab.
3. I promised my mother-in-law I would go wherever she went.
4. I gathered leftover grain in the fields of Boaz.
5. I married Boaz and became King David's great-grandmother.

## 13. Hannah (OT) · answer reference: 1 Samuel 1:27-28

1. My husband Elkanah had another wife, Peninnah.
2. Eli the priest thought I was drunk.
3. I prayed at Shiloh for a son, my lips moving without sound.
4. I gave my son to serve the Lord all his life.
5. I am the mother of the prophet Samuel.

## 14. Samuel (OT) · answer reference: 1 Samuel 3:10

1. I set up a stone and named it Ebenezer.
2. My sons Joel and Abijah took bribes, so the people asked for a king.
3. I anointed both Saul and David.
4. As a boy I served the Lord under Eli the priest.
5. God called my name in the night, and at first I thought it was Eli.

## 15. David (OT) · answer reference: 1 Samuel 17:49-50

1. I pretended to be insane in front of the king of Gath.
2. My great-grandmother was Ruth.
3. I was the youngest son of Jesse and took care of sheep.
4. I played the lyre to calm King Saul.
5. I defeated Goliath with a sling and a stone.

## 16. Solomon (OT) · answer reference: 1 Kings 3:9-12

1. I had 700 wives and 300 concubines.
2. The queen of Sheba came to test me with hard questions.
3. My mother was Bathsheba.
4. I built the first temple in Jerusalem.
5. I asked God for wisdom, and he made me the wisest king.

## 17. Elijah (OT) · answer reference: 1 Kings 18:36-38

1. I was a Tishbite from Gilead.
2. Ravens brought me bread and meat by a brook.
3. I brought the widow of Zarephath's son back to life.
4. I faced 450 prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel, and God sent fire.
5. I went up to heaven in a whirlwind, with a chariot of fire.

## 18. Elisha (OT) · answer reference: 2 Kings 2:9-14

2. I made an iron axe head float.
3. I told Naaman to wash seven times in the Jordan River.
4. I asked for a double portion of my master's spirit.
5. I picked up Elijah's mantle after he was taken to heaven.

## 19. Esther (OT) · answer reference: Esther 4:14

1. My Hebrew name was Hadassah.
2. My cousin Mordecai raised me after my parents died.
3. I was chosen as queen of Persia.
4. I asked the Jews to fast three days before I went to the king uninvited.
5. I exposed Haman's plot to destroy my people.

## 20. Nehemiah (OT) · answer reference: Nehemiah 6:15

1. I was cupbearer to King Artaxerxes.
2. Sanballat and Tobiah mocked and opposed my work.
3. I inspected the broken walls at night.
4. Some builders worked with one hand and held a weapon in the other.
5. I led the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls in 52 days.

## 21. Daniel (OT) · answer reference: Daniel 6:22

1. In Babylon I was given the name Belteshazzar.
2. I refused the king's food and ate vegetables with my three friends.
3. I read the writing on the wall for King Belshazzar.
4. I kept praying three times a day even when it was against the law.

## 22. Jonah (OT) · answer reference: Jonah 1:17

1. My father was Amittai.
2. I was angry when the plant that shaded me withered.
3. I paid the fare for a ship going to Tarshish.
4. Sailors threw me overboard to calm a storm.
5. A huge fish swallowed me, and later I preached in Nineveh.

## 23. Mary, mother of Jesus (NT) · answer reference: Luke 1:30-31

3. The angel Gabriel told me I would have a son.

## Worth a second look

- **Thomas, clue 2** ("we should go and die with him", John 11:16) and **clue 3** ("how could we know the way", John 14:5) are close paraphrases of what he said. Fine as clues, but they are the nearest thing to quoted words in the set.
- **Samuel, clue 1** (the Ebenezer stone, 1 Samuel 7:12) and **clue 5** (thought it was Eli calling, 1 Samuel 3) are accurate, but clue 1 is very hard for most groups.
- **Nicodemus, clue 1** says "about 75 pounds" of myrrh and aloes. That is the CSB wording (John 19:39); other translations say "a hundred pounds" or "litras".
- **Elisha, clue 1** "twelve teams of oxen" (1 Kings 19:19): he was plowing with the twelfth team, and the CSB says "twelve teams of oxen in front of him".
- **Joseph, clue 5** says "a special robe" on purpose; translations differ ("robe of many colors", "long-sleeved robe").
- **Abraham, clue 3** ("God made my name longer") refers to Abram becoming Abraham (Genesis 17:5).
- **Adam, clue 2** "third son named in Scripture" is Seth (Genesis 4:25); Genesis 5:4 says he had other sons and daughters.
- **Mary, mother of Jesus, clue 5** and **Hannah, clue 5** nearly give the answer. That is intended for the last clue, but swap them for something harder if you like.
- **Answer references** point to the key scene, not the first mention (for example Joshua 6:20 for Jericho, Jonah 1:17 for the fish; Jonah 1:17 is 2:1 in some Hebrew-numbered Bibles).
